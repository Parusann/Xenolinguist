# ADR 002: Deterministic truth outside the learner

Author: Parusan Natheeswaran

Status: implemented. A model-generated practice key may be useful entertainment, but cannot serve as its own scientific ground truth. Deterministic language generation and independent experiment renderers provide versioned meanings and withheld compositions.

Freeze source, prompts, inputs, information budgets and scoring before reading reserved results. Pass only visible observations and declared priors to each adapter. Retain errors, abstentions, tool failures and repeated attempts in the denominator. Fit/selection validation and final held-out evaluation have different roles.

Tradeoff: a bounded synthetic family is reproducible but not representative of natural-language diversity. Seed-disjoint vocabulary is weaker than structurally independent test languages. Published reports state that distinction and include sparse, irregular, contradictory and unavailable conditions.
